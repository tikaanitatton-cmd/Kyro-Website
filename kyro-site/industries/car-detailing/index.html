Marketing for car detailing businesses

Detailing is a visual trade, so the right ads sell themselves: a dull car on one side, a showroom finish on the other. We build campaigns that put those results in front of car owners in your suburbs and turn them into booked jobs.

What works for car detailing

  • Lead with the transformation. Before-and-after photos and short clips make the strongest detailing ads, so we build campaigns around your real jobs.
  • One offer per package. Interior, exterior and full details each get their own offer and landing page, so a visitor sees exactly what they came for.
  • Catch people when they search. Searches like car detailing near me and mobile car detailing are ready-to-book searches. Google Ads covers them.
  • Keep customers coming back. Retargeting past customers and website visitors brings people back for maintenance washes and add-on services.

Offers that work

  • A full-detail package priced clearly, with what's included listed
  • A first-visit offer, such as an interior refresh for new customers
  • Seasonal offers timed to when people care about their car, like before winter or after a long trip
  • A maintenance wash plan that turns one job into repeat visits

Creative that gets attention

  • Before-and-after photos taken in the same light and angle
  • A short time-lapse of a full detail
  • A close-up of paint after a clay bar and polish
  • A walk-around of the finished car, with the owner's reaction if they agree

Your first month

  • Week 1: set up tracking, pick one flagship package and build the first set of ads and a landing page
  • Weeks 2 to 3: test several creatives and audiences, and cut what isn't producing enquiries
  • Week 4: put more budget behind the winners and add retargeting for people who enquired or visited

Frequently asked questions

How many bookings can detailing ads bring in?

It depends on your area, budget and offer. In our published case studies, a Cairns detailer received 10 bookings in one week and a Newcastle detailer 18 bookings in two weeks.

Should I advertise mobile detailing differently?

Yes. Mobile detailers target a service radius rather than a fixed address, and the ad copy should say clearly which suburbs you cover.

What should I post to attract detailing customers?

Real transformations: before-and-after photos, time-lapses of a full detail and finished-car shots in good light.
