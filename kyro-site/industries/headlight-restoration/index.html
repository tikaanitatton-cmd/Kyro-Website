Marketing for headlight restoration businesses

Headlight restoration is quick, affordable and easy to show. The best campaigns catch drivers searching for a fix and bundle the service with a detail or inspection.

What works for headlight restoration

  • Win the search. People with yellowed headlights often search for a fix. Google Ads puts you at the top of those searches.
  • Show the difference. A cloudy lens next to a clear one is the entire pitch, and it works well as a short video or image.
  • Bundle it. Pairing restoration with a detail raises the value of each booking.
  • Keep the offer simple. A clear price and a quick turnaround time make it easy to say yes.

Offers that work

  • A fixed price for a pair, with the turnaround time stated
  • A bundle with a detail or an inspection
  • A same-day service for local drivers
  • A free assessment for cloudy or yellowed lenses

Creative that gets attention

  • A clear side-by-side of a yellowed and a restored headlight
  • A short before-and-after clip
  • A night shot showing the improved beam
  • The price and area on the image

Your first month

  • Week 1: set up call and form tracking and launch Google Ads for headlight restoration searches in your suburbs
  • Weeks 2 to 3: add a simple Meta ad with the before-and-after and test it against the search results
  • Week 4: add the bundle offer to lift the value of each booking

Frequently asked questions

Are ads worth it for a low-priced service like headlight restoration?

They can be, especially as an add-on that lifts the value of every booking. We keep the cost per enquiry low with tight local targeting.

What should the ad show?

A before-and-after of the lens, a clear price and your service area.

Can I bundle headlight restoration with other services?

Yes, and it is usually the best approach. A combined offer raises the average job value.
