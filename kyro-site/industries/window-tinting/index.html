Marketing for window tinting businesses

People shop for tint on heat, privacy and looks, and they want a quote quickly. We build campaigns that put those benefits in front of the right people and make asking for a quote easy.

What works for window tinting

  • Sell the benefits people care about. Heat reduction, UV protection, privacy and appearance each make a strong angle, and we test which one your customers respond to.
  • Make quoting simple. A short quote form or a message button removes friction. Fast replies win these jobs.
  • Separate car and home. Car tint and home tint attract different customers, so each gets its own campaign and tracking.
  • Show film brands and warranty. Naming your films and warranty on the ad and landing page builds trust before the first message.

Offers that work

  • A free quote with a fast reply promise
  • A package for a specific car type, such as a sedan or SUV, with film options listed
  • A heat and UV angle for hot months
  • A separate home-tint offer so that audience doesn't mix with car customers

Creative that gets attention

  • Side-by-side shots of a tinted and untinted window
  • A short clip showing the difference in heat or glare
  • The film brand and warranty on screen
  • A finished-car shot that shows the look

Your first month

  • Week 1: set up quote tracking, split car and home campaigns and launch one benefit-led ad for each
  • Weeks 2 to 3: test benefits such as heat, privacy and looks, and keep the one that brings quote requests
  • Week 4: scale the winner, speed up your reply time, and retarget visitors who didn't ask for a quote

Frequently asked questions

How much do tint enquiries cost with ads?

It varies by area and offer. For one tint client, we ran separate campaigns and averaged about $14.64 per messaging conversation across 468 enquiries.

Should car tint and home tint be advertised separately?

Yes. They are different customers with different questions, and separate campaigns let you see what each one costs and brings in.

What makes a good window tint ad?

A clear benefit such as heat or privacy, a real photo of your work and an easy way to ask for a quote.
