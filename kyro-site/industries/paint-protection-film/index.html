Marketing for paint protection film (PPF) installers

PPF is a high-value job for customers who care about their car. Our campaigns reach new-car owners and enthusiasts, show the quality of your installs and bring you serious enquiries.

What works for paint protection film (ppf)

  • Target new and high-value cars. Owners of new and premium vehicles are your best audience, and detailed targeting finds them.
  • Show installation quality. Close shots of clean edges and coverage options build the trust a high-ticket job needs.
  • Offer clear coverage packages. Front-end, partial and full coverage options help customers picture their choice and ask for the right quote.
  • Expect a higher cost per enquiry. PPF costs more per enquiry than lower-priced services. In one multi-service campaign, PPF enquiries cost $19.08 each against about $13 for tint.

Offers that work

  • Clear coverage packages, such as front-end or full-front, with what each covers
  • A new-car protection offer with a fast turnaround on the quote
  • A consultation before booking to qualify the enquiry
  • A bundle with ceramic coating or a detail

Creative that gets attention

  • Close shots of clean edges and film coverage
  • A before-and-after of a stone-chip-prone area
  • A short clip of an install in progress
  • A finished new car on the lot or in your studio

Your first month

  • Week 1: set up enquiry tracking and launch a campaign aimed at new and premium car owners in your area
  • Weeks 2 to 4: test coverage offers and creative, and add Google Ads for people searching for PPF
  • Ongoing: retarget visitors and video viewers, as this is a high-value decision that takes time

Frequently asked questions

What does it cost to get a PPF enquiry?

It depends on your area and offer. In one campaign we ran, paint protection enquiries cost $19.08 each, higher than tint, which is normal for a higher-priced service.

Who should PPF ads target?

Owners of new and premium cars, car enthusiasts and people near dealerships, within the suburbs you can serve.

Is it worth running Google Ads for PPF?

Often yes. People searching for paint protection film in their city are close to buying.
