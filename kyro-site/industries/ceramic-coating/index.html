Marketing for ceramic coating businesses

Ceramic coating is a considered purchase. Customers compare, ask questions and take their time, so the ads need to build trust and keep you in front of them until they book.

What works for ceramic coating

  • Show the finish. Close-up gloss, water beading and before-and-after shots are the proof that sells a coating.
  • Plan for a longer decision. Retargeting visitors and video viewers keeps you visible while they compare quotes.
  • Qualify the lead. Higher-priced work needs the right enquiries. Clear pricing signals and a short form keep the wrong leads out.
  • Capture search intent. People searching for ceramic coating in their city are close to buying, and Google Ads reaches them.

Offers that work

  • A coating package tiers list so customers can choose
  • A free paint assessment before booking
  • A care-and-maintenance add-on after the coating
  • An offer for new-car owners who want protection from day one

Creative that gets attention

  • Water beading on a freshly coated panel
  • Close-ups of gloss and depth
  • A short explainer on what the coating protects against
  • Before-and-after of a corrected, coated car

Your first month

  • Week 1: set up enquiry tracking, launch awareness video and a quote-request campaign
  • Weeks 2 to 4: keep showing your work to people who watched or visited, as this is a longer decision
  • Ongoing: send warm leads to a simple booking step and keep the creative fresh

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take for ceramic coating ads to bring bookings?

Interest can start within days, but this is a considered purchase. In our Perth case study, before-and-after content produced 28 bookings in 30 days.

Are ceramic coating leads more expensive?

Typically, yes, because the job is higher value. The aim is quality enquiries that turn into bookings, not the lowest cost per lead.

What content works for ceramic coating?

Close-up finish shots, water beading, before-and-afters and short explainer clips on the protection and care.
