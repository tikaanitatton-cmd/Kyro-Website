Marketing for car wash businesses

Car wash customers are local and they decide fast. We run ads that reach drivers within a few minutes of your site, and that turn first-time washes into regulars.

What works for car wash

  • Target drivers close to you. A car wash lives or dies on distance, so we focus tightly on the suburbs around your site.
  • Make the offer obvious. A clear price, a clear package and a clear way to book beat clever copy for a service people want quickly.
  • Show up for near me searches. Google search ads place you in front of drivers who are looking for a hand car wash right now.
  • Build repeat customers. Retargeting and simple loyalty offers turn a one-off wash into a regular visit.

Offers that work

  • A simple, clearly priced hand-wash package
  • A bundle that adds interior vacuum or tyre shine
  • A loyalty or multi-wash card for regulars
  • A weekday offer to fill quiet periods

Creative that gets attention

  • A before-and-after of a very dirty car
  • A satisfying clip of foam and rinse
  • Your team at work, which builds trust in a local business
  • A map-style graphic showing the suburbs you serve

Your first month

  • Week 1: set a tight radius around your site, set up message tracking and launch one clear offer
  • Weeks 2 to 3: test offers and creatives, and narrow the radius to the suburbs that respond
  • Week 4: scale the best ad and add a retargeting offer for people who messaged but didn't book

Frequently asked questions

Do Facebook ads work for a car wash?

They can, particularly when targeted to nearby drivers. In our Melbourne case study, a hand car wash received 60 messages in 20 days.

How far should I target around my car wash?

Most car washes get their best results close to the site. We test the radius and tighten it around the suburbs that actually book.

Can ads help with memberships or packages?

Yes. We can build a campaign for a wash package or membership offer and track how many people sign up.
