Audience targeting for automotive businesses

Showing an ad to the wrong people is the fastest way to burn budget. We build targeting that puts your detailing, tinting, coating or PPF offer in front of people who are likely to enquire, close enough to actually visit.

What we do differently

  • Targeting built around your real customers and your service area
  • Separate plans for new customers and for people who already know you
  • Constant refinement as the campaigns learn

How this helps you

  • Less budget wasted on the wrong audience
  • A lower cost per enquiry over time
  • A faster path to a profitable campaign

Our process

  • Define your service area and your most valuable customers
  • Build audiences from cold to warm, including website visitors and past customers
  • Test and refine using real enquiry data
  • Concentrate spend on the segments that book

Frequently asked questions

How do you choose who sees my ads?

We start with your service area and your best customers, then test audiences and keep the ones that produce enquiries.

What is retargeting?

Showing ads to people who have already visited your site or engaged with your page, so warm leads get a reminder to book.

Can you target by suburb?

Yes. Local businesses usually get the best results by focusing tightly on the suburbs they can actually serve.
