Google ads for automotive businesses

People who search for a service near them are often ready to book. Google Ads puts your business at the top of those searches. We set up and manage campaigns for automotive businesses so you pay for searches that can turn into jobs.

What we do differently

  • Campaigns built around the searches car owners actually make in your suburbs
  • Separate ad groups for each service, such as ceramic coating, PPF, tinting and headlight restoration
  • Call and enquiry tracking, so we count jobs and not only clicks

How this helps you

  • Reach customers at the moment they are looking for you
  • Pay for local searches, not for people far outside your area
  • Know which searches lead to enquiries

Our process

  • Research the local searches and competitors for each service
  • Build tightly focused campaigns and ads for each service
  • Track calls and form enquiries from day one
  • Trim wasted searches and put budget behind what converts

Frequently asked questions

Is Google Ads or Meta Ads better for car detailing and tinting?

They do different jobs. Google catches people who are already searching, while Meta puts your work in front of people before they search. Many businesses use both.

Can Google ads target only my suburbs?

Yes. We set the location, and add negative keywords, so your budget goes to searches from your area.

How do you track phone calls from Google Ads?

We set up call and form tracking so each enquiry is tied back to the search that produced it.
