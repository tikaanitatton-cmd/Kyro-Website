Frequently asked questions

How much should an automotive business spend on Meta ads?

Most clients start between $500 and $3,000 a month in ad spend. The right figure depends on your area, your services and how many jobs you can take on.

Do Meta ads bring phone calls or messages?

Both are possible. We set each campaign to the action that suits your business, such as Messenger or Instagram messages, calls, or a quote form, and track it from day one.

Which services work best on Facebook and Instagram?

Anything visual does well: detailing transformations, tint and PPF installs, ceramic coating finishes and headlight restorations. We build the creative around your real work.
