Privacy policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

What we collect

When you apply through our form we collect the details you give us: your name, phone number, the type of business you run, your ad budget range and any message. We also collect basic website analytics.

How we use it

We do not sell your personal information.

  • To reply to your application and discuss our services.
  • To understand how visitors use the site, so we can improve it.

Who handles it

Form submissions are sent to us through Web3Forms, a form processing service, and arrive by email. Site analytics are provided by Google Analytics, which uses cookies or similar technology to measure visits.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time by emailing kyro.marketing@outlook.com. You can also block analytics cookies in your browser settings.
