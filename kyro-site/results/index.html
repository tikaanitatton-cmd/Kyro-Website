Real ad results from real campaigns

Screenshots from Meta Ads Manager, with the numbers explained in plain English.

468 people messaged a tint business across three campaigns

We ran three campaigns for one client, one for each service: home window tinting, paint protection film and car window tinting. Together they brought in 468 message enquiries at an average of $14.64 each.

The car tint and home tint campaigns both came in around $13 per enquiry. Paint protection cost more per enquiry ($19.08), which is normal for a higher-priced service.

61 leads and 75 conversations from one campaign

One campaign brought 61 leads at $15.63 each, plus 75 people messaging the business.

1,347 people clicked the ad at 71 cents per click, and 3.42% of people who saw it clicked, which is well above most of the other results here.

5% of people who saw this ad clicked it

This ad had the highest click rate in this set. 5.11% of people who saw it clicked, about 1 in every 20.

29 of its 30 clicks went straight through to the business, at about 70 cents each. Each person saw it 1.61 times on average, so it grabbed attention without being shown over and over.

5 people messaged the business from a $40 test

This was a small test with $39.88 of ad spend. For that money, 5 people started a chat with the business, which works out to $7.98 per conversation.

Meta also recorded about 4 leads at $9.97 each.

15 message enquiries across two ads for $147

Two ads ran side by side (one per row). The first spent $21.58 and brought in 2 conversations. The second spent $125.90 and brought in 13 conversations at $9.68 each.

Running ads side by side shows which one deserves more of the budget.

9 leads and about 15 conversations from $176

$175.93 of ad spend brought 179 clicks. Meta recorded 9 leads at $19.54 each.

The cost per messaging conversation was $11.73, which works out to about 15 people messaging the business.

25 people messaged the business while the ad was still running

This screenshot was taken while the campaign was still live. At that point, 25 people had started a conversation at $14.80 each, about $370 in total.

Meta had also recorded 16 leads.

28 conversations and 19 leads from $426

$425.62 of ad spend brought 476 clicks, which is under $1 per click.

28 people messaged the business at $15.20 each, and Meta recorded 19 leads.

32 people reached out from under $500 of ads

$489.88 of ad spend brought 32 message conversations at $15.31 each.

Meta also recorded about 21 leads at $23.33 each. Some of these may be the same people who messaged.

20 leads and 24 conversations across two ads

Two ads ran together for $411.49 in total. The first spent $79.04 for 3 leads and 6 conversations.

The second spent $332.45 for 17 leads and 18 conversations. More people clicked it too (3.29% against 2.31%), so it was the clear winner.

23 leads at $18 each, with one of the best click rates

$418.81 of ad spend brought 23 leads at $18.21 each and 24 conversations at $17.45 each.

3.56% of people who saw the ad clicked it, one of the highest rates in this set.

8 conversations at under $11 each

A smaller ad spent $87.74.

It brought 8 conversations at $10.97 each and 3 leads at $29.25 each.

53 clicks, but no phone calls yet

This ad was set up to get people to call the business directly. After $42.27 it had 53 clicks at $0.80 each, but no calls yet (shown by the dash under cost per result).

Tests like this show which approach to drop, so the budget goes to the ads that bring in bookings.

5 leads at $11 each

$55.29 of ad spend brought 110 clicks at 50 cents each.

It produced 5 leads at $11.06 each, one of the cheapest costs per lead in this set.

Four ads tested to see which one grabs attention

Four versions of an ad (one per row) ran on a very small budget to see which one people respond to. The top ad got 15 clicks at 10 cents each.

Another got 17.86% of viewers clicking, but from a small sample. The winners get the bigger budget.

11 conversations, the cheapest at $6.29 each

Two ads ran side by side. The first brought 7 conversations at $6.29 each, the lowest cost per conversation in this set. The second brought 4 at $12.91 each.

Together that's about $96 for 11 people messaging the business.
