What we check

Ten things that decide whether a customer books or scrolls past.

  1. Does your bio say what you do and where?
  2. Is there one clear link to book or enquire?
  3. Do your first nine posts show results, not just pretty cars?
  4. Are before and after shots clear, with the service and price range?
  5. Do you answer the questions customers always ask?
  6. Is your Google Business Profile current (photos, hours, categories)?
  7. Are you replying to reviews and messages quickly?
  8. Is there a pinned post explaining how to book?
  9. Do your Reels and Stories show the work in progress?
  10. Can a new customer get a quote in under a minute?
