FAQ

Targeting questions

How do you choose who sees my ads?

We start with your service area and your best customers, then test audiences and keep the ones that produce enquiries.

What is retargeting?

Showing ads to people who have already visited your site or engaged with your page, so warm leads get a reminder to book.

Can you target by suburb?

Yes. Local businesses usually get the best results by focusing tightly on the suburbs they can actually serve.
