FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How quickly can we get started?

Most clients are live within 5-7 business days of onboarding, once we have access to your ad accounts and any brand assets.

Do you work with automotive businesses?

Yes, that's our focus: car detailing and washing, window tinting, ceramic coating, PPF and headlight restoration businesses.

Can you promote a specific service, like ceramic coating or PPF?

Yes. We build a separate campaign for each service, so every offer gets its own ads, targeting and tracking, and you can see which ones bring in the most jobs.

Which platforms do you manage?

Meta, TikTok, and Google Ads by default, with ad creative and website support available alongside any of them.

Is there a minimum ad spend?

No strict minimum, though most clients see the strongest results starting around $500-$3,000 per month in ad spend.

Do you require long-term contracts?

No. Engagements are month-to-month, so you're never locked in past the point it's working for you.

How do you report on performance?

Weekly check-ins plus a clear monthly summary tied to revenue and ROAS, not just clicks or impressions.

Whether you run a detailing studio, a car wash, a tint shop or a ceramic coating and PPF business, we build the ads that put your work in front of local car owners and turn them into booked jobs.
