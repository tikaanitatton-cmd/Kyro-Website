Window tint & paint protection client · 3 campaigns   Biggest result

468 people messaged a tint business across three campaigns

We ran three campaigns for one client, one for each service: home window tinting, paint protection film and car window tinting. Together they brought in 468 message enquiries at an average of $14.64 each.

The car tint and home tint campaigns both came in around $13 per enquiry. Paint protection cost more per enquiry ($19.08), which is normal for a higher-priced service.
