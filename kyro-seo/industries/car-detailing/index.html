FAQ

Car Detailing advertising questions

How many bookings can detailing ads bring in?

It depends on your area, budget and offer. In our published case studies, a Cairns detailer received 10 bookings in one week and a Newcastle detailer 18 bookings in two weeks.

Should I advertise mobile detailing differently?

Yes. Mobile detailers target a service radius rather than a fixed address, and the ad copy should say clearly which suburbs you cover.

What should I post to attract detailing customers?

Real transformations: before-and-after photos, time-lapses of a full detail and finished-car shots in good light.
