FAQ

Window Tinting advertising questions

How much do tint enquiries cost with ads?

It varies by area and offer. For one tint client, we ran separate campaigns and averaged about $14.64 per messaging conversation across 468 enquiries.

Should car tint and home tint be advertised separately?

Yes. They are different customers with different questions, and separate campaigns let you see what each one costs and brings in.

What makes a good window tint ad?

A clear benefit such as heat or privacy, a real photo of your work and an easy way to ask for a quote.
